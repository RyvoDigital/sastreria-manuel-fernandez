## 1. Executive Summary

SastrePro is a **B2B SaaS platform** designed specifically for tailors, ateliers, and custom clothing workshops. It is **not a direct competitor** to Sastrería Manuel Fernández, which operates as a **B2C luxury tailoring brand**. Instead, SastrePro serves the *business management* needs of tailoring professionals, while Sastrería Manuel Fernández serves the *end customer* seeking bespoke garments.

The comparison is analogous to comparing a luxury fashion brand with Shopify — they serve entirely different purposes within the same broader industry.

## 3. Pricing Structure

SastrePro operates on a **freemium + subscription** model:

| Plan | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free Plan** | €0 | Basic functionality, limited client management, essential features to get started |
| **Monthly Plan** | €29/month (VAT incl.) | Full feature access, unlimited clients, cloud measurements, PDF quote generation, priority support. **7-day free trial included.** |
| **Annual Plan** | €290/year (VAT incl.) | Everything in Monthly + significant savings, priority access to future updates. **Best value option.** |

**Key pricing insight:** The annual plan offers approximately **17% savings** compared to paying monthly (€290 vs €348/year). The 7-day free trial reduces friction for new sign-ups.

| Aspect | Detail |

## 7. Competitive Positioning

### SastrePro's Strengths:
1. **Niche focus** — Built specifically for tailoring/couture (not generic CRM)
2. **Freemium entry** — Free plan allows discovery without risk
3. **Dual affiliate model** — Both income generation and subscription reduction options
4. **Cloud accessibility** — Measurements from any device is a strong selling point
5. **Professional PDF generation** — Appeals to image-conscious artisans

### SastrePro's Weaknesses:
1. **Thin content** — About and Contact pages are essentially empty
2. **No social proof visible** — No testimonials, case studies, or client logos on homepage
3. **Generic branding** — Feels more like a template than a crafted brand experience
4. **No team/story** — Missing the human element that luxury services require
5. **Cookie banner blocks content** — Poor UX for first-time visitors

## 9. Strategic Insights & Opportunities

### What Sastrería Manuel Fernández Can Learn from SastrePro:

1. **Trial Before Commitment**
   - SastrePro offers 7-day free trial → Sastrería Manuel Fernández already offers free video call consultations. This is actually *more* valuable than a software trial.
   - **Opportunity:** Emphasize the "no-risk discovery" aspect more prominently.

2. **Professional Documentation**
   - SastrePro generates elegant PDF quotes → Sastrería Manuel Fernández could enhance client communications with beautifully designed digital proposals/measurement sheets.
   - **Opportunity:** Create branded digital documents for quotes, fittings, and care instructions.

3. **Referral Incentives**
   - SastrePro's €10/referral model → In luxury tailoring, word-of-mouth is already the primary acquisition channel.
   - **Opportunity:** Formalize a referral program (e.g., "Recommend a friend, both receive a complimentary accessory" or fitting credit).

4. **Cloud/Accessibility Narrative**
   - SastrePro sells "access from any device" → Sastrería Manuel Fernández sells "your garment, crafted for you, wherever you are."
   - **Opportunity:** The 3D models and video call features already address remote accessibility. Continue building this narrative.

### What SastrePro Does NOT Do (Sastrería Manuel Fernández's Moat):

1. **Brand Heritage & Story** — SastrePro has no story. Sastrería Manuel Fernández has decades of craftsmanship legacy.
2. **Sensory Experience** — Software cannot replicate the touch of fabric, the fitting room experience, or the personal relationship.
3. **Exclusivity** — SastrePro wants mass adoption. Sastrería Manuel Fernández thrives on exclusivity and limited capacity.
4. **Emotional Connection** — A wedding suit, a ceremonial garment — these carry emotional weight that software cannot replicate.

## 10. Threat Assessment

**Direct Threat: NONE**

SastrePro does not compete for the same customers. A person looking for bespoke tailoring services will not choose between "buying a suit" and "buying tailor management software."

**Indirect Considerations:**
- If SastrePro grows significantly, they may expand into a marketplace connecting tailors with clients (similar to Treatwell for beauty). This would be worth monitoring.
- Their affiliate program could potentially recruit tailoring influencers who might otherwise promote individual tailoring brands.

---

## 12. Conclusion

SastrePro is a well-positioned B2B SaaS for the tailoring industry with a solid freemium model and an interesting dual-purpose affiliate program. However, it poses **zero direct competitive threat** to Sastrería Manuel Fernández.

Instead, SastrePro represents the *tooling layer* of the industry — the software that tailors use to run their businesses. Sastrería Manuel Fernández operates in the *premium client experience layer* — the front-facing brand that clients choose for quality and craftsmanship.

The most valuable takeaway is not competitive defense, but **inspiration**: SastrePro's emphasis on digitization, professional presentation, and referral incentives are concepts that can be adapted to enhance the client experience at Sastrería Manuel Fernández, not to compete with them.
